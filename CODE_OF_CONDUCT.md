# Code of Conduct

BleepBloop is made by and for a lot of different people. We want everyone who shows up here, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives, to feel welcome and safe.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source, to code, or to English.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you assume.
- Give feedback about the work, never about the person.
- Make room: notice who hasn't spoken yet, and credit other people's ideas.
- Respect people's names and pronouns.

## What isn't okay

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or jokes at the expense of any group.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Discrimination of any kind, including against women, trans and nonbinary people, people of colour, disabled people, or anyone for their religion, age, body, class or where they come from.
- Sharing someone's private information without their consent.
- Repeatedly pushing past a "no".

## If something happens

Email [hello@pitch.dog](mailto:hello@pitch.dog). We'll read it, keep it confidential, and get back to you. We may remove content, or ask someone to step away from the project for a while or for good. We'll always take the side of the person who was harmed over the comfort of the person who caused it.

This applies in all of the project's spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

This code is our own, written in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
