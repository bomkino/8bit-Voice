## Ideas we learned from

The character creator's art and code are original; none is copied. Its ideas owe a lot to open-source avatar makers that came before it: [DiceBear](https://www.dicebear.com) (whose Pixel Art style is CC0), [notion-avatar](https://github.com/Mayandev/notion-avatar), [react-nice-avatar](https://github.com/dapi-labs/react-nice-avatar), [boring-avatars](https://github.com/boringdesigners/boring-avatars), and Pablo Stanley's Avataaars and Open Peeps. [Picrew](https://picrew.me) showed how much fun a maker can be. The one-tap light came from seeing Magnific's Character builder, which colours a character's lighting in one tap.

The voices owe their spirit to the sound chips of the NES, the Game Boy and 80s arcade machines, and to the talking text of games like Animal Crossing and Undertale. No sounds from any game are used.
