Make anything talk like a video game.

Type a line, pick a character, hear them say it. Then save it as a video for Reels, TikTok, Shorts or YouTube. It all happens on your device, works offline, and it’s free and open source.

Delivery

Tap a delivery to add it to the line you’re on, or type it in brackets: (slow, awed), (very fast and very high). Punctuation does the rest: a question goes up, a dash cuts off, capitals are louder. WIZARD: Hello puts a name on one line. Ctrl Enter plays the line you’re typing; outside the box, Space plays and ← → step through the lines.

    Make it from the command line

    The same engine runs in Node, so this gives the same sound, sample for sample. Clone the repo and run it in its folder.

    Get the app

    The same tool in its own window, for macOS, Windows and Linux. It works offline, and your lines and settings stay on your computer.

    The apps aren’t signed by Apple or Microsoft, so your computer asks once before opening one. Here’s what to click. Every version is on the releases page.

    Every sound is synthesized here, on your device, from square, triangle and wavetable oscillators, a little model of a throat, formant filters and noise. There are no samples, so there is nothing to license, and what you make is yours. The same line, voice and take always give the same clip. Made by pitch.dog, open source under the MIT License.
