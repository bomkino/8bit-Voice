8-bit Voice

Type a line and hear it as a video-game voice. The words come out as gibberish, but the rhythm, the melody and the attitude survive, so whoever answers can tell the audience what was said.

Type a line to hear it.

Lines

Drop a text file: one line per clip
Delivery

Tap a delivery to add it to the line you’re on, or type it in brackets: (slow, awed), (very fast and very high). Punctuation does the rest: a question goes up, a dash cuts off. Ctrl Enter plays the line you’re typing; outside the box, Space plays and ← → step through the lines.

    Get the app

    The same tool in its own window. It works offline, and your lines and settings stay on your computer.

    The apps aren’t signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so your computer asks once before opening one. Here’s what to click. Every version is on the releases page.

    Every sound is synthesized here, on your device, from square, triangle and wavetable oscillators, formant filters and noise. There are no samples, so there is nothing to license. The same line, voice and take always give the same clip. Made by pitch.dog for Typecast.
